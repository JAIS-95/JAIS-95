## What I'm working on

Protein target prediction for microbial natural products. A multimodal model combining chemistry, biosynthetic gene cluster and protein-target representations to predict which protein a natural product acts on. It is evaluated with leakage-controlled splits against strong similarity baselines, and used to ask which genomic information actually helps.
"Paper in preparation"

* My work sits at the intersection of **genomics**, **machine learning**, and **bioinformatics**, where I develop scalable pipelines, predictive models, and tools to accelerate scientific research.
